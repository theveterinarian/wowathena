FAQ

Honest answers to common questions.

Is Athena state-approved?

Yes. Our DUI Alcohol or Drug Use Risk Reduction Program is certified by the Georgia Department of Driver Services under RRP Cert #10432, and our Driver Improvement course is state-approved as well. Our anger management classes and clinical evaluations are separate services — check your court order for exactly what you need, or call us and we'll help you read it.

Will DDS accept my DUI school certificate?

Yes. Certificates of completion from our DDS-certified Risk Reduction Program (RRP Cert #10432) are accepted by the Georgia DDS for license reinstatement. If you're unsure what your court or DDS requires, call us at 706.215.9661 before you register.

How long does the DUI Risk Reduction Program take?

The program has two parts: an assessment and a 20-hour course taught over several sessions. Most students finish within about a week. Classes run on a set schedule, live online or in person — register online or call to see our next class dates.

Can I start class the same day I register?

Not usually. You can register and complete your DDS paperwork online at any time, and then you'll join our next scheduled class. Call us and we'll help you find a date that fits your schedule.

Can I take classes online?

Many of our classes are offered live online or in person at our Athens office. Online classes are taught live by an instructor — Georgia DDS does not accept certificates from self-paced online DUI courses. Some steps may need to be completed in person, so call us and we'll tell you exactly what applies to you.

Do I need a clinical evaluation?

Georgia requires a clinical evaluation to reinstate your license after two or more DUI convictions within 10 years, and a court may order one in other cases. Our clinical evaluation is $150. If the evaluation recommends treatment, that treatment must be completed as well.

What is Driver Improvement used for?

Georgia's 6-hour Driver Improvement course can satisfy a court order, remove up to 7 points from your driving record once every 5 years, meet certain license-reinstatement requirements, and may qualify you for an insurance discount.

How does Class Now, Pay Later work?

Qualifying students can take class now and pay later through Stripe. Ask about it when you register or call us at 706.215.9661.

Do you send my certificate to the court?

When needed, yes — at no extra cost. When you complete your program, we can send your certificate to your probation officer, court clerk or attorney. You also receive your own copy.

What are your office hours?

Our office at 110 Athens West Parkway, Ste. C, Athens, GA 30606 is open Monday through Friday, 10am–6pm. We're available in person or by phone at 706.215.9661, or email registration@wowathena.com.
