FAQ

Honest answers to common questions.

Will the court accept my certificate?

Yes. Athena is a Georgia State-approved DUI/RRP provider, and our certificates are accepted by all 159 counties in Georgia and by the Department of Driver Services for license reinstatement. If you're unsure about your specific court, call us at 706.215.9661 and we'll verify before you pay.

How fast can I complete the 20-hour DUI/RRP course?

Most students complete the full 20-hour intervention course in 3–7 days at their own pace through our virtual option. In-person classes follow a set schedule — view available dates on the enrollment page or call us.

Can I complete classes 100% virtually?

Yes for most programs — DUI/RRP, anger management, parenting, and family enrichment can be completed entirely virtually. FVIP requires some in-person sessions per Georgia law. You'll need a webcam for identity verification.

How does Class Now, Pay Later work?

We partner with Stripe to offer interest-free payment plans — split your tuition into 2 or 4 installments. No credit check required. You start the course as soon as your first payment clears.

Do you send my certificate to the court?

Yes — at no extra cost. As soon as you complete your program, we email and mail your certificate directly to your probation officer, court clerk, or attorney. You also receive your own copy.

What are your office hours?

Our office at 110 Athens West Parkway, Ste.C is open Monday through Friday, 10am–6pm. Virtual classes run evenings and weekends as well. Call 706.215.9661 or email registration@wowathena.com anytime.

Are your instructors qualified?

Every instructor is state-certified for the courses they teach. Our FVIP facilitators are licensed counselors. Our DUI Risk Reduction instructors hold GA DDS certification. Meet our team on the staff page.
