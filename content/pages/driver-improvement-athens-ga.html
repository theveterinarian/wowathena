Georgia's Driver Improvement course is a 6-hour defensive driving class approved by the Georgia Department of Driver Services (DDS). You can use it for a court order, to take up to 7 points off your license, to reinstate after certain suspensions, or to ask your insurer about a discount. Athena DUI Academy teaches the state-approved course in Athens.

DDS treats "Driver Improvement" and "Defensive Driving" as the same thing, so if a judge or DDS letter uses either name, this is the course they mean. It's classroom instruction only. There is no behind-the-wheel driving.

Why people take Driver Improvement

DDS lists five reasons to take the course. Drivers from Athens-Clarke County and the surrounding Northeast Georgia area can take it with us for any of them.

How point reduction works in Georgia

Georgia adds points to your license when you're convicted of certain traffic violations. DDS says, for example, that speeding less than 15 mph over the limit does not add points. Under O.C.G.A. § 40-5-86, licensed Georgia residents can have up to 7 points removed once every 5 years by completing a certified Driver Improvement course.

After you finish the course, DDS gives you two ways to request the reduction:

  • Bring your original Certificate of Completion to a DDS Customer Service Center, or
  • Mail the original certificate to the Georgia Department of Driver Services, P.O. Box 80447, Conyers, Georgia 30013.

Because you can only use point reduction once every five years, think about timing. You can check how many points are on your license through your DDS Online Services account.

Reinstating after a points suspension

DDS suspends a license when a driver gets 15 points in 24 months. For a first points suspension at age 21 or older, DDS says you're eligible to reinstate as soon as you complete an approved defensive driving course. The reinstatement fee for a first points violation is $200 by mail or $210 in person, paid to DDS.

Two uses, one certificate. If you took the course to reinstate and also want points removed, tell the DDS Customer Service Center when you hand in your certificate. DDS says you have to ask for both at that time.

Reinstatement rules differ for second and third suspensions, drivers under 21 and other suspension types. Check your own record in DDS Online Services, which shows step-by-step requirements. If you're dealing with a DUI instead, see how to get your license back after a DUI in Georgia, and note that a DUI needs a different course, DUI school, not Driver Improvement.

Can you take Georgia defensive driving online?

Be careful here. DDS says it does not recognize online Driver Improvement courses for driver's license issues, and certificates from online courses are not accepted for any purpose related to your license or driving privileges. DDS also says courses that are not DDS-approved can't be used for license issues, even if they're well known.

Georgia's rules do separately allow a certified clinic, with DDS's prior approval, to teach live remote classes in real time on a set platform at a set time. That is not the same as a self-paced online course.

What the class is like

The course runs 6 hours and focuses on safe driving attitudes and behavior. It's taught by a certified instructor in a classroom setting.

Attendance rules are strict at every certified clinic. DDS says students who arrive late can expect to be denied entry, and students who are absent or leave early may have to start over at full cost. Look at your schedule before you sign up.
