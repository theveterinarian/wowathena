How much is DUI school in Georgia?

DUI school costs $360 at every DDS-certified program in Georgia. Here's where that money goes, why the price never changes, and the other costs that usually come with a DUI.

By Athena DUI Academy & Family EnrichmentLast reviewed September 28, 20266 min read

Key takeaways

  • DUI school (the DUI Alcohol or Drug Use Risk Reduction Program) costs $360 at every DDS-certified program in Georgia.
  • The $360 is split into a $100 assessment, a $235 20-hour class and a $25 workbook.
  • Your license reinstatement fee is separate and goes to DDS: $200 by mail, $210 in person for a first DUI at age 21 or older.
  • If you have two or more DUI convictions in 10 years, you will also need a clinical evaluation, and possibly treatment.
  • DDS does not accept certificates from online DUI courses, so a cheaper online class can cost you more in the end.

DUI school in Georgia costs $360. That's the price at every program certified by the Georgia Department of Driver Services (DDS), whether you take it in Athens, Atlanta or Savannah, live online or in person. The state sets the amount, so no certified school can charge you more or less.

The official name for DUI school is the DUI Alcohol or Drug Use Risk Reduction Program, often shortened to "Risk Reduction" or "RRP." If a judge or DDS has told you to complete it, this is the class they mean.

Where the $360 goes

DDS breaks the price into three parts. You pay for all three, and you need all three to earn your Certificate of Completion.

PartCostWhat it is
Assessment component$100A 130-question screening that looks at your alcohol and drug use and how it affects your driving.
Intervention component$235The 20-hour class itself, taught over several sessions in a group setting.
Workbook$25The course workbook you use during class.
Total$360The same at every DDS-certified program in Georgia.

Why every school charges the same price

Because DDS sets the price, shopping around won't save you money. What does change from school to school is everything else: the class schedule, whether you can attend live online or in person, how easy registration is, and whether you can reach a real person when you have a question about your court order.

Those differences matter more than they sound. A class schedule that fits around your job means you finish sooner and get back on the road sooner. If you're comparing schools, compare them on that.

Watch out for "cheap" online DUI classes. The Georgia DDS says certificates from online DUI, alcohol or drug use risk reduction courses can't be accepted for anything related to your driver's license. If a website offers a self-paced online DUI class for less than $360, it isn't a Georgia-certified Risk Reduction Program. Live virtual classes taught in real time by a certified school are a different thing, and they cost the same $360.

What the $360 does not cover

DUI school is usually one cost among several. Here are the others people most often ask about, so none of them catch you off guard.

A first DUI at age 21 or older brings a 12-month license suspension, but DDS lets you apply to reinstate after 120 days if you've finished DUI school and paid the reinstatement fee. Our guide to getting your license back after a DUI in Georgia walks through each step.

If this is your second DUI or more within 10 years, plan for a clinical evaluation as well. We explain how that works in Second DUI in Georgia: the clinical evaluation.

Does the price change for a second DUI or a non-DUI charge?

No. DDS lists one price for the Risk Reduction Program, $360, and that's what you pay whenever you're required to take it. That includes a second DUI, and it includes the other offenses that require the program: possession of illegal drugs, underage possession of alcohol while driving, and Boating Under the Influence. Our guide to drug possession, underage alcohol and BUI explains how those cases work.

What changes with a repeat DUI is everything around the class. Two or more DUI convictions within 10 years means a clinical evaluation before you can reinstate, and treatment if the evaluator recommends it. The suspension rules and reinstatement steps differ too, so check the specific rules for your situation before you budget.

How to keep the cost manageable

  • Ask about paying later. At Athena, qualifying students can take class now and pay later through Stripe.
  • Pay the DDS fee by mail, or online if you're eligible. For a first DUI at 21 or older, the mail rate is $10 less than paying in person.
  • Only pay once. Make sure your program is DDS-certified before you pay. A certificate DDS won't accept means paying for the class a second time.
  • Pick a schedule you can keep. Choosing a class time that fits your week makes it far less likely you'll miss a session and have to sort out a makeup.

Frequently asked questions

Is DUI school cheaper if I take it online?

No. Every DDS-certified Risk Reduction Program charges the same $360. Live virtual classes from a certified school cost the same as in-person classes. Be careful with self-paced online DUI courses: the Georgia DDS does not accept certificates from online DUI, alcohol or drug use risk reduction courses for anything related to your license.

Does the $360 include the assessment?

Yes. The $360 covers the $100 assessment, the $235 20-hour intervention course and the $25 workbook. You complete the assessment and the course to earn your Certificate of Completion.

Is the license reinstatement fee part of the $360?

No. The reinstatement fee is paid to the Georgia DDS, not to the school. For a first DUI at age 21 or older it is $200 if you pay by mail or $210 in person. DDS also offers the lower rate online when you're eligible.

Can I pay for DUI school in installments?

At Athena DUI Academy, qualifying students can take class now and pay later through Stripe. Ask about it when you register or call 706.215.9661.

Do I also have to pay for a clinical evaluation?

Only if it applies to you. Georgia requires a clinical evaluation to reinstate your license after two or more DUI convictions within 10 years, and a court can order one in other cases. Athena's clinical evaluation is $150.
