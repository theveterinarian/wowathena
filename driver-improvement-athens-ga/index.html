Driver Improvement course in Athens, GA

Georgia's Driver Improvement course is a 6-hour defensive driving class approved by the Georgia Department of Driver Services (DDS). You can use it for a court order, to take up to 7 points off your license, to reinstate after a points suspension, or to ask your insurer about a discount. Athena DUI Academy teaches the state-approved course in Athens.

DDS treats "Driver Improvement" and "Defensive Driving" as the same thing, so if a judge or DDS letter uses either name, this is the course they mean. It's classroom instruction only, with no behind-the-wheel driving. DDS also says it is not the same as Driver's Education, the separate program 16-year-olds take for a Class D license.

DDS lists five reasons to take the course. Drivers from Athens-Clarke County and the surrounding Northeast Georgia area can take it with us for any of them.

How point reduction works, step by step

  1. Check your points. Create a DDS Online Services account or use the DDS 2 GO app to see your license status and current points.
  2. Confirm you qualify. Under O.C.G.A. § 40-5-86, licensed Georgia residents may have up to 7 points removed once every 5 years. If you used a reduction in the last five years, DDS won't grant another.
  3. Take the course at a certified clinic. DDS only accepts certificates from clinics on its certified list, and under Rule 375-3-3-.05 a certificate used for reinstatement must show the course was taken after the violation date.
  4. Get your original Certificate of Completion. Photocopies won't do. DDS asks for the original.
  5. Request the reduction. Bring the original certificate to a DDS Customer Service Center, or mail it to the Georgia Department of Driver Services, P.O. Box 80447, Conyers, Georgia 30013.
  6. Confirm on your record. Log back into Online Services to see the reduction posted.

Because you can only use point reduction once every five years, use it when it keeps you under the 15-point line.

Reinstating after a points suspension

For a first points suspension at age 21 or older, DDS says you're eligible to reinstate as soon as you complete an approved defensive driving course; until you do, the suspension stays in place indefinitely. A limited permit may be available during a first or second points suspension, but DDS says none is available for a third.

Mail payments go to DDS Validation Unit, P.O. Box 80447, Conyers, Georgia 30013, with your Official Notice of Suspension and the certificate. In person, DDS accepts cash and cards but not checks, and you'll need proof of identity.

Two uses, one certificate. If you took the course to reinstate and also want points removed, tell the DDS Customer Service Center when you hand in your certificate. DDS says you have to ask for both at that time.

Your record in DDS Online Services shows the step-by-step requirements for your case. If you're dealing with a DUI instead, see how to get your license back after a DUI in Georgia. A DUI needs a different course, DUI school, not Driver Improvement.

Can you take Georgia defensive driving online?

Be careful here. DDS says it does not recognize online Driver Improvement courses for driver's license issues, and certificates from online courses are not accepted for any purpose related to your license or driving privileges. DDS also says courses that are not DDS-approved, and it names AARP as an example, can't be used for license issues even though they're well known.

Georgia Rule 375-5-1-.09 separately allows a certified clinic, with DDS's prior approval, to teach through web services such as Zoom, as long as the instruction is synchronous: real-time teaching and interaction in a specific virtual place at a specific time. That is not the same as a self-paced course you click through alone.

Using the course for points, reinstatement or court? Tell us your reason when you call, so we can confirm the right class format before you pay. In-person class at our Athens office is the most direct choice for license and court purposes.

What happens if…

You arrive late or miss part of the class

DDS's FAQ answers this with the case of a student turned away for being five minutes late: yes, the clinic can do that. Late students can expect to be denied entry and may have to start over at full cost, and the same goes for leaving early. Refunds and make-ups are between you and the clinic under your contract.

You take a self-paced online course

The certificate won't count for your license, points or reinstatement, and you'll pay again for a certified class.

You took the class before the ticket

For reinstatement, Rule 375-3-3-.05 says the certificate must show the course was taken and completed after the violation date that caused the suspension. A class from last year won't lift this year's suspension.

You drive while suspended

DDS's Driver's Manual lists driving while suspended as an offense that requires DDS to suspend your privileges again. If a limited permit is available for your suspension, get it before you drive.

Common mistakes

  • Sending a photocopy. DDS asks for the original certificate for points reduction.
  • Forgetting to ask for both uses. Reinstatement and points reduction must be requested together when you hand in the certificate.
  • Using the 5-year reduction on 2 points. You get one reduction every five years, so use it when it matters.
  • Assuming a nolo plea erases everything. DDS accepts one nolo contendere plea for a moving violation in any 5-year period without points; a second one gets points even for a different offense.
  • Waiting past three years for a replacement. Clinics only keep records for three years. After that you retake the course.

Special situations

Under 21

DDS suspends the license of any driver under 21 convicted of a single 4-point violation, such as reckless driving or speeding 24 mph or more over the limit, and drivers under 18 at 4 points within 12 months. Check your record in Online Services for the steps, and see Georgia DUI under 21 for alcohol offenses.

Super Speeder

Speeding at 75 mph or more on a two-lane road, or 85 mph or more anywhere, triggers a $200 Super Speeder fee from DDS in addition to any points. You have 120 days from the notice to pay, or DDS suspends the license and adds a $50 reinstatement fee.

Out-of-state drivers

DDS does not assess points against non-Georgia residents, but Georgia residents get points for out-of-state violations. If you hold a Georgia license and got a ticket elsewhere, the point reduction and the Georgia course still apply to you.

Key terms

  • Points: penalties DDS adds to your record on conviction of certain violations; DDS's published table runs from 1 to 6 per offense.
  • Points reduction: DDS removing up to 7 points once every 5 years after a certified course.
  • Points suspension: the suspension DDS imposes at 15 points in 24 months.
  • Certificate of Completion: the original document a certified clinic issues; DDS needs the original.
  • Nolo contendere: a "no contest" plea; DDS accepts one for a moving violation in five years without assigning points.
  • Limited driving permit: a restricted permit DDS may issue during a suspension for work, school, medical care and similar trips; $32 for up to a year.

Frequently asked questions

How many points does defensive driving take off in Georgia?

Up to 7 points, and only once every 5 years. You have to be a licensed Georgia resident and finish a DDS-certified Driver Improvement course. Then you take the original certificate to a DDS Customer Service Center or mail it to DDS, P.O. Box 80447, Conyers, Georgia 30013, to request the reduction.

How many points until your license is suspended in Georgia?

DDS suspends a Georgia license at 15 or more points within 24 months, counted from violation date to violation date. Drivers under 21 are suspended for any single 4-point conviction, and drivers under 18 for 4 points within 12 months. You can check your current points in DDS Online Services.

How much does the Georgia defensive driving course cost?

DDS says certified clinics must charge $95 when the course is taken for license reinstatement, points reduction or court purposes. For other reasons, such as an insurance discount, a clinic may charge up to $95. Call Athena at 706.215.9661 to confirm your price and the next class date.

Is defensive driving the same as Driver Improvement in Georgia?

Yes. DDS says the two names are interchangeable. Both refer to the same 6-hour classroom course taught at DDS-certified Driver Improvement clinics, with no behind-the-wheel driving.

Can I take the Georgia 6 hour defensive driving course online?

DDS says it does not recognize online Driver Improvement courses for driver's license issues and won't accept those certificates for anything related to your license or driving privileges. Georgia's rules do let a certified clinic teach live, real-time remote classes with DDS approval, which is different from a self-paced course. Ask the clinic which format meets your requirement.

How long does it take to get points removed after the class?

DDS doesn't publish a processing time. You control the first part: the reduction is requested by handing in or mailing the original certificate. After that, check your record in DDS Online Services to see when the points come off.

Will a defensive driving course lower my car insurance?

It might. DDS lists insurance premium reduction as one reason to take the course and points to O.C.G.A. § 33-9-42 for the qualifications. Ask your insurance company whether you or members of your household qualify before you count on a discount.

Can I use one certificate for both reinstatement and points?

Yes, if you say so at the time. When you hand in your Certificate of Completion at a DDS Customer Service Center, tell them you want it used for both license reinstatement and points reduction. DDS says you have to ask for both then.

What if I lose my Driver Improvement certificate?

Contact the clinic where you took the class. DDS allows a replacement fee of up to $5, and clinics keep records for only three years. If the clinic has closed and you took the class within three years, call DDS at 678-413-8745. After three years you would have to retake the course.

Does a nolo contendere plea add points in Georgia?

DDS's Court Reference Manual says DDS will accept one nolo contendere plea for a moving violation in any 5-year period without assigning points. A second nolo plea in that window gets points even if it's a different offense, and some offenses don't allow nolo to avoid points at all. Your attorney can tell you whether it fits your case.
