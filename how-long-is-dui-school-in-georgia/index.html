How long is DUI school in Georgia?

DUI school in Georgia is 20 hours of class plus a short assessment, and most people finish within a week. Here's how the time is split up, the deadlines that apply, and how it lines up with getting your license back.

By Athena DUI Academy & Family EnrichmentLast reviewed September 28, 20267 min read

Key takeaways

  • Georgia DUI school is a 20-hour class (the Intervention Component) plus a 130-question assessment taken first.
  • DDS says the whole course takes less than a week in most cases.
  • State rules require every session to be completed within four weeks, with no more than two sessions in one day.
  • Your assessment is valid for one year. If you don't enroll in the class within that year, you'll need a new assessment and a new fee.
  • For a first DUI at 21 or older, you can apply to reinstate after 120 days of a 12-month suspension if you have your Certificate of Completion and pay the fee.

DUI school in Georgia is a 20-hour class plus a 130-question assessment, and most people finish the whole thing in less than a week. The 20 hours are split into several sessions, and state rules say all of them must be completed within four weeks, with no more than two sessions on any one day.

The official name is the DUI Alcohol or Drug Use Risk Reduction Program. The hours are the same at every certified school in Georgia, and the same for live online and in-person classes. What changes from school to school is how those hours are laid out across the week.

How many hours of DUI school are required?

DUI school has two parts, and you need both to earn your Certificate of Completion.

PartWhat it isTime
Assessment ComponentA 130-question screening about your alcohol and drug use and how it affects your drivingDone before the class starts
Intervention ComponentThe class: therapeutic education and peer group counseling in a group setting20 hours, over several sessions
Meal breaksRequired when two sessions are held on the same dayAt least 1 hour, on top of the 20 hours

Georgia's rules require every student to attend the class "for the entire 20 hours," and no one may be admitted late or dismissed early from any session. There's no shortcut. The school must also have your assessment processed at least 30 minutes before the first session begins.

How many days does DUI school take?

DDS says that "in most cases, the entire 20-hour course roughly takes less than a week." The exact number of days depends on the school's schedule, because the rules allow up to two sessions a day. A school may spread the sessions across more days, or hold two in one day with a meal break between them.

The rules that shape every schedule:

  • No more than two sessions in a single day. Together with the 20-hour total, that's why DDS describes the class as several days of lessons.
  • All sessions within four weeks. Programs must schedule the class so every session is completed inside that window.
  • Sessions in order. DDS policy says you must attend all sessions in sequence, and you can't go on to the next session after missing one.
  • Same class, start to finish. Students from one class can't be mixed into another to finish sessions, except to make up an excused absence.

Before you sign up, look at every session date and time, not only the first one. DDS advises taking any scheduling conflicts into account before you sign a contract to attend.

How long do you have to finish DUI school?

Three timelines can apply, and it helps to keep them separate.

  1. Your court's deadline. If a judge ordered you to complete DUI school, your sentencing paperwork may say by when. That deadline comes from your court, not from DDS or the school. If you can't tell what your order requires, ask your attorney or the court.
  2. Your assessment's shelf life. The assessment is valid for one year. DDS policy says that if you don't enroll in the class within that year, you'll need another assessment and another assessment fee.
  3. The four-week class window. Once your class starts, the school must schedule all sessions within four weeks.

Your license is the other clock. It stays suspended until you meet DDS's reinstatement requirements, and after a DUI your Certificate of Completion is one of them. Waiting to take the class usually means waiting longer to drive legally.

How DUI school fits the 120-day reinstatement for a first DUI

For a first DUI in five years at age 21 or older, DDS suspends your license for 12 months. After 120 days, you can apply for reinstatement if you meet the conditions, which include presenting a Certificate of Completion from a DDS-approved Risk Reduction Program and paying the reinstatement fee.

StepTiming
License suspended (first DUI in 5 years, age 21+)12 months
Earliest you can apply to reinstateAfter 120 days, if all conditions are met
DUI school (assessment plus 20-hour class)Usually less than a week
Certificate issued by the schoolWithin 1 business day, no more than 48 hours, after you finish
Reinstatement fee paid to DDS$200 by mail or online, $210 in person

Because the class itself only takes about a week, the thing most likely to hold you up at day 120 is not having started. Having your certificate ready before you reach that date means you can apply as soon as you're eligible. If you're thinking about starting before your case is resolved, read whether you can start DUI classes before your court date and check with your attorney.

If you refused the state's chemical test, the rules are different: DDS says a refusal brings a suspension of at least one year and makes you ineligible for a limited driving permit if you're convicted of a first DUI. What if you're late or miss a session?

This is where most delays come from. DDS says students who arrive late to any session "can expect to be denied entry," and students who are absent or leave early may need to start the course over again at full cost. Refunds and makeups are between you and your school, under the contract you sign.

The rules make one exception. If your absence is excused, you may attend another class to make up the sessions you missed. An absence counts as excused if you're called to military duty, you have a medical emergency or one involving an immediate family member, or there's a death in your immediate family. The school owner may excuse other absences at their discretion.

Live online classes count the same way. In a live virtual class you must be logged in on time, on camera for every session and stay until the end. A dropped connection or a camera switched off can cost you a session. See how live online DUI classes work in Georgia.

How long until you get your certificate?

Georgia's rules require the school to issue your Certificate of Completion "within one business day, but no more than 48 hours," after you successfully complete all program requirements. Before it's issued, your instructor gives a post-test and confirms you've met every course requirement.

Keep the certificate somewhere safe. If you lose it, the school that taught your class can issue a replacement for a fee of up to $20. Schools only have to keep student records for three years, so don't count on getting a copy many years later.

Does a second DUI add time?

Yes. If you have two or more DUI convictions within 10 years, DDS requires proof of a state-approved clinical evaluation before it will reinstate your license, and the evaluation may recommend treatment. Plan for that on top of DUI school. We explain the process in Second DUI in Georgia: the clinical evaluation.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours is DUI school in Georgia?

The class portion is 20 hours, and you must attend all of it. You also take a 130-question assessment before the class starts. When two sessions fall on the same day, there's a meal break of at least an hour that doesn't count toward the 20 hours.

Can you finish DUI school in 2 days in Georgia?

Don't plan on it. The 20 hours are split into several sessions, state rules allow no more than two sessions in a single day, and DDS describes the class as several days of lessons. Most people finish in less than a week.

How long do I have to complete DUI school after a DUI in Georgia?

It depends on your case. Your court order may set a deadline, and your license stays suspended until you meet DDS's reinstatement requirements, so check your paperwork and ask your attorney, the court or DDS if you're unsure. Once you start, the school must schedule all sessions within four weeks.

How soon do you get your DUI school certificate in Georgia?

Georgia rules require the school to issue your Certificate of Completion within one business day, and no more than 48 hours, after you finish all program requirements. If you lose it later, the school can issue a replacement for a fee of up to $20.

What happens if I miss a DUI school session?

You may have to start the course over and pay the full cost again, because students can't be admitted late or leave early. If your absence was excused, such as military duty, a medical emergency or a death in your immediate family, the rules let you make up the missed session in another class.
